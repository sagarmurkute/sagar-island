Capabilities

Engineered for Windows.
Designed for Delight.

Every micro-interaction is tuned with custom cubic-bezier spring physics to feel native, responsive, and tactile.

🎵

SMTC Media Control

Synchronizes instantly with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and browser audio. Displays track info, dancing 4-bar equalizer, seek bar, and full playback controls.

🔊

WASAPI Hardware HUDs

Hooks directly into Windows audio endpoints. Replaces intrusive default flyouts with a modern, unobtrusive floating pill with instant mute alerts.

☀️

WMI Brightness Tracking

Monitors internal and external display brightness adjustments in real-time with smooth progress transition bars.

🔋

Win32 Battery Monitor

Live battery telemetry, charging status, and critical power alerts with intelligent threshold triggers.

📋

Smart Clipboard Companion

Detects copied text, URLs, file lists, and images in real-time. Gives you an instant glanceable confirmation with quick actions.

🛡️

Top-Bezel Edge Wakeup

Auto-hides during inactivity to stay out of your workflow. Move your cursor to the top bezel to instantly wake the island.
